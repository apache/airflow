Full probe configuration for scheduler in Helm chart

The scheduler ``livenessProbe``, ``startupProbe`` and the new ``readinessProbe``
(disabled by default) now support an ``enabled`` flag and ``httpGet``, ``tcpSocket``
and ``grpc`` handlers in addition to the default ``exec`` handler, as well as
``successThreshold`` and ``terminationGracePeriodSeconds``.
