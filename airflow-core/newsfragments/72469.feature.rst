Added ``[execution_api] workload_token_expiration_time`` to let operators decouple the lifetime
of the workload JWT token (sent with a queued task to its executor) from
``[scheduler] task_queued_timeout``. Previously, raising ``task_queued_timeout`` to tolerate a
longer queue backlog also delayed the scheduler's orphaned-queued-task detection by the same
amount, since both were controlled by the same value. When left unset, the new option falls
back to ``task_queued_timeout``, preserving existing behavior.
