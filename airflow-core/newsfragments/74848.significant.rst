Datadog timer metrics are now reported in milliseconds

With the Datadog (DogStatsD) metrics backend, ``Stats.timer()`` metrics such as
``scheduler.scheduler_loop_duration`` were sent in seconds, while they are documented in milliseconds
and reported in milliseconds by the StatsD and OpenTelemetry backends. They are now sent in
milliseconds, so their values become 1000x larger after upgrading. Update Datadog dashboards, monitors
and SLOs that use these timers accordingly.
